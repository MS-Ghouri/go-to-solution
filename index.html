Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working together, timelines, and the development process.

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. A straightforward custom theme typically takes 2–4 weeks. Full custom websites with plugin development and e-commerce usually take 4–8 weeks. During the planning phase, I'll provide a detailed timeline based on your specific requirements.
At minimum: a clear description of what you need, any existing branding or design assets, and your content (text, images). If you have wireframes or a design file, that helps too. If you're not sure about the details yet, that's fine — we can figure it out during the discovery phase.
Yes. I can work with existing WordPress installations — whether that means adding new features, fixing issues, rebuilding the theme, or migrating to better hosting. I'll review your current setup and advise on the best approach.
Post-launch support covers bug fixes, minor adjustments, and guidance on using your site. It does not include new feature development or significant design changes — those would be scoped as a separate project or retainer.
